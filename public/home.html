SKYCOT

Spliz

A video studio that lives on your Mac. Put an idea on the board, get a storyboard you can read from, record once, drop the files in, and Spliz hands back a finished cut with captions and the upright clips for Shorts and TikTok. The transcript is the editor: delete a word and the cut is made. Your own AI agent checks the recording and proposes chapters and show notes, and nothing it writes gets past the no-slop gate. Nothing is uploaded, no accounts, no keys. Built in four days with Claude, Codex and Cursor, improved over 49 blind review rounds.

Argo

AI teammates you could talk to and hand work to. You described a teammate in plain English and Argo built it, a person approved anything sensitive, and every action went into a tamper evident audit log. A Rust core, Claude and OpenAI models, voice through ElevenLabs and Deepgram, connectors to Slack, Gmail, Notion and HubSpot. AI coding agents built it under my direction in 57 days: 2,901 merged pull requests. It ran live in production in June 2026, before Grok Bot, Muse and Dots. Open source, with the full story on the Argo page.

LVLme: for the job you can't get yet

LVLme works out honestly how far you are from the job you want, turns the gap into things you can go and do, and measures again as you climb. It checks every open role at the organisations you choose, gathers evidence from sources you approve, runs a recruiter screen and a consistency check of your CV, LinkedIn and website, and re-scores the plan as you act on it. Runs inside Claude Code or Codex on your own computer. Open source, MIT, sixteen public releases in its first six days. I use it on my own reach.

mStack

My open source collection of skills for people and their AI agents, agent agnostic and MIT licensed. outcome-first settles the goal and every question up front, then runs to a proven result; multi-evaluator improves a product in rounds with AI reviewers from different makers; design-direction builds whole working directions to choose from; ping-me and away-mode keep me in the loop from my phone; crew runs work in the background. Written for Claude, Codex or Cursor. I use it on this site.

About

I'm not a developer. My background is aerospace and aviation, and in every role the job was the same: find where a complicated system isn't working, work out what's actually true, and fix the cause.

Now I use the same method with AI. I strip a problem back to what's actually true, write it down so clearly that an agent can't misread it, then let a team of AI agents build it while I check the results like an engineer would. I'm writing up what I learn as I go, from Melbourne.
