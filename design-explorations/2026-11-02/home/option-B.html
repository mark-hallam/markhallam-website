WPT 02Hard problems

Hard problems I've solved

Most of my career has been spent on problems where getting it wrong grounds aircraft. These are the ones I'm proudest of.

  1. 01LFBOWSSS

    Bringing ten A320s into Australia.

    Tigerair, 2007 to 2014.

    Every aircraft needed regulator approvals, paperwork and a delivery slot before it could carry a passenger. I held all three, through to the airline's last A320 in 2014.

  2. 02

    Writing the rules a new airline flew by.

    Tigerair, 2007.

    When Tigerair Australia started up, I wrote its maintenance documents, including the aircraft maintenance schedule, minimum equipment list and configuration deviation list.

  3. 03YPAD

    A maintenance base from a blank page.

    Tigerair.

    Took the Adelaide line maintenance base from tender to regulator approval, doubling the maintenance ports in the network.

  4. 04

    Finding fuel in the engines.

    Tigerair.

    An engine wash program recovered 2 to 3 percent engine performance and saved more than $500,000 a year in fuel.

  5. 05NZAA

    Moving every maintenance record without losing one.

    Tigerair, 2015.

    Migrated the fleet's maintenance data from SIA Engineering's SAP system to Air New Zealand: the records that prove every aircraft is safe to fly.

  6. 06

    Phones on, gate to gate.

    Tigerair, 2014.

    Ran the program that won CASA approval for passengers to use their devices from boarding to arrival.

    Australian Aviation, September 2014
  7. 07

    New rules for twin-engine jets.

    Tigerair, 2015.

    Moved the fleet onto CASA's new extended diversion time rules (EDTO), which set how far a two-engine jet may fly from a suitable airport.

  8. 08

    Selling through COVID.

    PPG Aerospace, 2020.

    My best sales month came in May 2020, at 133 percent of the year before.

  9. 09YLTV

    A hydrogen drone, legal to fly.

    Swinburne AIR Hub, 2022 to 2023.

    Set up the university's drone operator's certificate and was the person accountable to CASA for it. Worked through the rules for carrying hydrogen gas, and held the schedule and budget for Hydrogen to the Skies.

    Watch: Hydrogen to the Skies
  10. 10

    A $2.7 billion air traffic system.

    Airservices Australia, now.

    I turn CMATS, Australia's new civil and military air traffic system, into real benefits for the people who fly: user preferred routes, a single flight data region, wake turbulence management, and conflict detection tools.

WPT 07About

About

I'm not a developer. My background is aerospace and aviation, and in every role the job was the same: find where a complicated system isn't working, work out what's actually true, and fix the cause.

Now I use the same method with AI. I strip a problem back to what's actually true, write it down so clearly that an agent can't misread it, then let a team of AI agents build it while I check the results like an engineer would. I'm doing all of this in public, from Melbourne.
